RECOVERY PROCESS

What happens after a vehicle goes missing.

A clear, sequential response your F&I team can walk a customer through at the point of sale.

01

Theft occurs

The hidden unit runs on its own battery, so it keeps reporting even if the thief disconnects the vehicle battery.

02

Customer reports the vehicle stolen

The customer files a police report, then calls the 24/7/365 Safelink recovery line with the report number.

03

Recovery team engages

Cellular and GPS/satellite data are used to track the vehicle in real time, coast-to-coast.

04

Direct coordination with law enforcement

The recovery team works directly with police to recover the vehicle quickly and safely.

05

Guarantee backs the outcome

If the vehicle isn't recovered, the money-back guarantee protects the customer's investment, subject to the written program terms.

Questions dealers ask

Straight answers for GMs, F&I directors and dealer principals. Anything else, ask us on a consult.

What is Safelink Theft Recovery?

Safelink is a hidden GPS theft-recovery system for vehicles. Dealers sell it as a one-time F&I menu product with no monthly fees, and Safelink's 24/7/365 recovery team works with law enforcement when a protected vehicle is stolen.

How do dealers make money on Safelink?

The dealer buys the unit at dealer cost and sells it on the F&I menu at a retail price the store sets, keeping the difference as gross. Use the calculator above with your own volume and gross, and we will give you dealer cost on a 15-minute consult.

Is there a monthly fee for the customer?

No. Safelink for vehicles is a one-time purchase with no monthly or yearly subscription.

How long does installation take?

About 60 seconds. The unit mounts magnetically in a concealed location with no wiring, no drilling and no external antenna, so it needs no bay time.

What does the dealer dashboard show?

Every Safelink-equipped unit reports into the dealer dashboard: live location, battery health, stock status and geofence alerts for unauthorized movement, on or off the lot.

Which dealerships is Safelink for?

Franchise, independent, and powersport and motorcycle dealers. Advanced Automotive Concepts in Lake Forest, California, is an authorized Safelink agent serving all three.

What happens if a protected vehicle is stolen?

The customer files a police report and contacts Safelink's 24/7/365 recovery line. The device switches into a high-frequency recovery mode and the recovery team coordinates directly with police. A money-back guarantee applies under Safelink's written program terms.
