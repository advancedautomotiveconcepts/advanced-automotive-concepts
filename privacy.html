Privacy notice

What this website collects, where it goes, and how to reach us about it.

What we collect

This site collects information only when you send it to us through the inquiry form: your name, email address, and anything else you choose to include, such as your phone number, dealership, role, monthly volume, and message.

How it is used

We use what you send to reply to your inquiry and to follow up about the products you asked about. We do not sell it.

Where it goes

Form submissions are delivered to our business email by FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service, which processes the submission in order to send it. Our email is hosted on Microsoft 365.

Online purchases

When you buy online, payment is taken on a secure checkout page run by our payment provider. Your card details go to the provider, not to us; we receive your name, contact details, shipping address, and what you ordered, so that we can fulfil it.

Cookies and third-party content

This site does not set its own cookies. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Videos are loaded from YouTube only after you press play, and links to our social media accounts and online store take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email mlarsen@advancedautomotiveconcepts.net or call (949) 324-1208.
