How it works

Three steps, and most riders only ever do the first one.

  1. Install it

    Slide the tracker into the end of the handlebar and seat it with the rubber grommet, or drop it into the seat tube. It takes about a minute.

  2. Ride as usual

    The TruePoint GPS app runs in trip mode for everyday use. The battery lasts two years with no charging.

  3. If it's stolen

    File a police report, turn on recovery mode in the app, then contact Safelink support and share the location updates with police.

Questions riders ask

The short answers. Shipping, returns and renewal details are on the shipping and returns page.

What is the Safelink Bicycle GPS (GLS11)?

A hidden GPS theft-recovery tracker for bicycles and e-bikes. It is about the size of a lip-balm tube and slides inside the handlebar or seat tube, so a thief has no reason to look for it.

How much does it cost, and is there a subscription?

$199, plus shipping and any sales tax shown at checkout. There is no monthly subscription. Two years of service are included, and a paid renewal is available after that.

How long does the battery last?

The battery lasts two years and never needs recharging.

How is it different from a Bluetooth tag?

A Bluetooth tag updates only when a compatible phone passes nearby. The GLS11 uses its own GPS, so it does not rely on nearby phones, and it comes with 24/7/365 recovery support that works with police.

How do I install it?

It takes about a minute. The tracker slides into the end of the handlebar and is held by a rubber grommet, or it can go in the seat tube. No wiring and no special tools.

What do I do if my bike is stolen?

File a police report, turn on recovery mode in the TruePoint GPS app, then contact Safelink support and share the location updates with police.

Can I return it?

Yes, within 30 days of delivery if it is unused, not activated and in its original packaging. Full details are on the shipping and returns page.
