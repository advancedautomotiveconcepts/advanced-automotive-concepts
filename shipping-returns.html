Shipping, returns & tax

The terms for products bought online from Advanced Automotive Concepts. Dealer program orders are covered by the dealer's own agreement.

Last updated November 2026

Shipping

We ship within the United States by UPS. The shipping charge is shown at checkout before you pay and is added to your order total.

Orders ship within five business days. You will receive an email with tracking when your order leaves. If we cannot ship within that time, we will tell you and you may cancel for a full refund.

Please check the shipping address at checkout. If a package comes back to us because the address was wrong or delivery was refused, we will refund the product price once it arrives; the original shipping charge is not refundable.

Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout from your shipping address, where the law requires us to collect it, and is shown before you pay.

Cancelling an order

You may cancel for a full refund any time before your order ships. Call or email us with your receipt.

Returns

You may return a product within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the product price if it is unused, has not been installed or activated, and is in its original packaging.

  • Contact us first so we can give you return instructions.
  • Return shipping is at your cost, and the original shipping charge is not refundable, unless the product arrived damaged, defective, or was not what you ordered.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method within ten business days of the return reaching us.
  • A device that has been activated cannot be returned for a change of mind, because its service term has started.

Damaged, defective, or wrong item

If your order arrives damaged, does not work, or is not what you ordered, tell us within 30 days of delivery. We will replace it or refund it in full, including shipping, and cover the cost of sending it back.

Service term and renewal

The Safelink Bicycle GPS (GLS11) includes two years of service with no monthly subscription. Continued service after two years requires a paid renewal. Renewal pricing will be posted on this page.

Recovery guarantee and warranty

The theft-recovery guarantee and any product warranty are provided by Safelink Theft Recovery under its written program terms. Ask us for the current terms before you buy, and contact us if you need to make a claim; we will help you through it.
